# 🚀 Revenue Growth Engine: Customer Segmentation & ROI Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
Every business sits on a goldmine of transaction data, but most are "leaking" revenue because they don't know which customers are about to leave forever. 

This project transforms **500,000+ raw retail records** into a strategic decision-making tool. By applying **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis**, I identified over **$1,030,000 in "At-Risk" revenue** and developed a data-driven action plan to protect profit margins and maximize Marketing ROI.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Python (Pandas, Datetime):** For data engineering, cleaning 500k+ rows, and feature engineering.
* **Excel (Pivot Tables, Advanced Formulas):** For building the executive-facing dashboard and behavioral segmentation.
* **Data Modeling:** RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Segmentation logic.

---

## 💡 The "Revenue Boost" Strategy
This roadmap tells a manager **exactly where to spend the marketing budget** for the highest possible return:

| Segment | Revenue Impact | Recommended Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Champions** | $1.08M | **$0 Cost Growth:** Offer exclusive VIP access/status rather than discounts. |
| **At-Risk** | **$1.03M** | **Immediate Rescue:** Deploy targeted "We Miss You" campaigns to recover lost profit. |
| **Loyal** | $2.83M | **Up-selling:** Suggest premium products based on their high frequency. |
| **Newbies** | High Potential | **Retention:** Automated follow-up 14 days after purchase to guarantee a 2nd visit. |

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Results
* **Revenue Protection:** Identified that a significant portion of total revenue ($1M+) is at risk of churning in the next 30 days.
* **Margin Optimization:** Proved that the business can stop sending broad discounts to "Champions," saving thousands in unnecessary marketing costs.
* **Data-Driven Decisions:** Transitioned the business from "guessing" to a visual dashboard that updates in real-time.
